# Brooks Walker

Construction owner-operator (heavy civil, excavation, framing, project management) who builds AI systems that check their own work.

I direct AI the way I'd run a crew. I set the job, the standards and the checks; the AI builds; and nothing counts until it's verified.

## What I build
- **A governed multi-agent AI system** across 16 professional domains: legal research, construction estimating, engineering, land survey, the trades and more. Each domain has a quality gate the agents can't bypass.
- **AI legal research with hard verification.** Before anything goes out, each cited authority is checked four ways: it exists, the quote matches the source, it fits the facts, and it's used fairly. Fabricated citations are blocked, not just flagged.
- **Construction estimating tools:** a cost database, bid math, and a plan-takeoff tool that measures quantities straight from PDF plans.
- **Measured, not claimed.** I test the verification against public citation-hallucination datasets and track what it misses as well as what it catches.
